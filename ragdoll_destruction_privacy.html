Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile application Ragdoll Destruction Playground (Package Name: com.Ragdoll.Playground.Sandbox.Physics.RagdollDestructionPlayground, hereinafter referred to as the "Game"), developed and maintained by Alexander Gellert / Tiny Heart Studios (hereinafter referred to as "we", "us", or "our").

We are committed to protecting player privacy. This policy outlines what data is collected, how it is used, and your privacy choices when playing Ragdoll Destruction Playground.

1. Information Collection and Processing

The Game does not require mandatory user accounts or direct collection of personally identifiable information (such as your real name, address, or email address).

To support game performance, ad monetization, and platform analytics, the Game incorporates authorized third-party Software Development Kits (SDKs). These third-party providers may automatically collect certain device and network metadata:

  • Advertising Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (GAID) or IDFA;
  • Device & System Data: Hardware model, operating system version, screen resolution, and system language;
  • Network Data: IP address and generalized location (country/city level);
  • Performance & Diagnostic Data: Frame rate, game crashes, and advertisement interaction logs.

2. Third-Party Services and Monetization

The Game integrates third-party services to deliver contextual or personalized advertisements and track technical stability:

  • Google Play Services: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google AdMob / AdSense: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543
  • Unity Ads (if applicable): https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

3. How We Use the Data

The collected technical data is used solely to:

  • Serve contextual and targeted advertisements within the sandbox gameplay;
  • Diagnose client-side physics crashes, latency, and stability issues across device hardware;
  • Comply with platform regulations and anti-fraud protections.

4. Children’s Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K)

The Game is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or 16 in the European Economic Area). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through third-party services, please contact us immediately to have it removed.

5. User Rights & Advertising Opt-Out

You can reset or restrict the collection of your Advertising ID directly in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads → Reset/Delete advertising ID).
