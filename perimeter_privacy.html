Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile application Perimeter: 3D Forest Survival (Package Name: com.ths.survival.forest.shelter.monsters, hereinafter referred to as the "Game"), developed and maintained by Alexander Gellert / Tiny Heart Studios (hereinafter referred to as "we", "us", or "our").

The Game is built using Godot Engine 4. We respect player privacy and are dedicated to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what data is collected, how it is used and processed, and the choices and rights available to you.

1. Information Collection and Processing

The Game does not require account registration, and we do not collect or store direct personally identifiable information (PII) such as your legal name, home address, or phone number.

The Godot 4 engine executes game and survival logic on your device. However, to support game analytics, gameplay balancing, in-app purchases, and advertisement monetization, the Game integrates verified third-party Software Development Kits (SDKs). These providers may automatically collect non-personal technical data and device identifiers:

  • Advertising Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (GAID) or ID for Advertisers (IDFA);
  • Gameplay & Telemetry Data: In-game milestones, survival duration, shelter upgrades, crafting events, and difficulty balancing metrics collected through GameAnalytics;
  • Device & System Data: Hardware model, operating system version, CPU/GPU architecture, screen resolution, and system language;
  • Network & Location Data: IP address and coarse geographic region (country or city level);
  • Diagnostic & Performance Data: Frame rates, memory utilization, application stability, and crash logs.

2. Integrated Third-Party Services

We partner with trusted third-party service providers to deliver ads, process purchases, and analyze game performance. Each provider operates under its respective privacy terms:

  • GameAnalytics: Collects anonymized gameplay telemetry to analyze survival mechanics, progression balance, and player retention.
    https://gameanalytics.com/privacy/
  • Unity Ads: Serves contextual, interstitial, and rewarded video advertisements.
    https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy
  • Google Play In-App Billing (IAP) & Google Play Services: Powers secure digital goods purchases, achievements, and cloud saves.
    https://policies.google.com/privacy

3. In-App Purchases (IAP) & Financial Information

The Game offers optional in-app purchases (such as virtual items, currency, or removal of advertisements). All financial transactions and payment processing are handled entirely and securely through the Google Play In-App Billing API.

We do not have access to, collect, or store your credit card information, bank account details, or billing address. We only receive purchase confirmation tokens and product identifiers to validate and deliver your acquired in-game items.

4. How We Use the Data

The technical data collected by integrated services is used exclusively to:

  • Deliver, grant, and verify in-game items and currency purchased via Google Play Billing;
  • Present rewarded video and interstitial advertisements via Unity Ads;
  • Analyze survival gameplay balance, difficulty curves, shelter upgrades, and retention through GameAnalytics;
  • Diagnose client-side bugs, performance drops, and unexpected crashes across various Android devices;
  • Ensure platform compliance, anti-cheat integrity, and legal obligations.

5. Children’s Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K)

The Game is designed for general audiences and is not directed towards children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe that a minor has provided personal details through integrated third-party services, please reach out to us and we will promptly ensure the data is deleted.

6. User Rights & Privacy Controls

You have options to manage and limit the collection of your data at any time:

  • Advertising ID Reset / Opt-Out: You can reset or delete your Google Advertising ID on your Android device (Settings → Google → Ads → Reset/Delete advertising ID).
  • GameAnalytics Opt-Out: You can learn more about GameAnalytics privacy practices and user rights at https://gameanalytics.com/privacy/.
  • Google Play Account Data: You can manage or delete your Play Games profile and associated game data through Google Play Games settings.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may periodically revise this Privacy Policy to reflect updates in game features, SDK integrations, or applicable legal requirements. The latest version will always be published on this page with the "Last updated" date.
